The standards and regulatory authorities behind Glen's manufacturing — each badge shown with the body or standard behind it.

ISO 9001:2015

The international quality management standard from the International Organization for Standardization — certification focuses on how consistently processes meet customer and regulatory requirements.

Quality Management

DRAP Registered

Pakistan's federal regulator for therapeutic goods, under the Ministry of National Health Services Regulations & Coordination — overseeing registration, safety and quality of health products.

Regulatory Compliance

PCSIR Tested

Pakistan's public-sector research organisation, providing independent laboratory testing and analysis that backs product safety with documented results.

GMP Certified

An internationally recognised manufacturing standard covering hygiene, equipment, process control and documentation — so products are consistently produced to a defined quality.
